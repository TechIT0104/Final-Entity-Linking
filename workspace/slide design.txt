Design a modern, professional PowerPoint presentation template with a clean white background and a bold geometric corporate style inspired by premium business decks. The design should feel unique, polished, energetic, and boardroom-appropriate — not boring, not overly minimal, and not too flashy.

Use a vibrant yet professional color palette built around teal, deep teal, turquoise, mustard yellow, and small hints of light gray or navy. The design should rely on sharp diagonal shapes, layered corner elements, clean geometric panels, and subtle abstract accents to create a strong visual identity. Keep the layout bright and airy, but make sure the colors feel confident and modern rather than soft or pastel.

Visual Style:
White or very light background across all slides
Strong geometric corner overlays in teal and yellow
Diagonal blocks, angled ribbons, and layered shapes
Minimal dotted patterns or thin line decorations for texture
Clean modern corporate aesthetic
Slightly dynamic and stylish, but still formal and trustworthy
Color Theme:
Primary Teal: deep, rich teal for major blocks and title text
Accent Yellow: mustard/golden yellow for contrast and highlights
Secondary Turquoise / Aqua: for supporting shapes
Neutral White and Light Gray for balance
Optional very small touches of dark navy for typography
Avoid black backgrounds completely
Avoid neon colors and over-saturation
Typography:
Use a bold, clean sans-serif font for titles, such as Montserrat, Poppins, or League Spartan
Use a simple readable sans-serif font for body text, such as Open Sans, Lato, or Inter
Make titles large, confident, and centered or left-aligned depending on the slide
Keep body text short, clear, and structured
Layout Style:
Use asymmetrical but balanced composition
Place the title in a clean central area with strong framing shapes around the edges
Use large empty white space in the center for clarity
Add decorative elements mostly in corners and borders so the slide does not feel cluttered
Keep slide content easy to scan from a distance
Design Elements:
Geometric triangles, diamonds, angled strips, and layered corner shapes
Thin line connectors, small dots, and minimal icon accents
Optional abstract building/architecture visuals or subtle business imagery
Small highlighted labels or number markers for agenda slides
Charts and diagrams should match the same teal/yellow theme
Slide Types to Include:
Title Slide with strong corner geometry and big centered title
Agenda Slide with numbered items and clean icons
Section Divider Slide with a large section number and bold shape framing
Content Slide with text blocks, icons, and one featured image or visual
Chart/Data Slide with simple modern bar or donut charts in the same color palette
Conclusion / Thank You Slide with minimal elegant layout and balanced corner shapes
Overall Feel:

The presentation should look like a professional business, startup, consulting, or academic board presentation, but with a modern creative edge. It should feel smart, confident, and visually memorable.

Important Rules:
Keep the design consistent across all slides
Do not make it too plain
Do not make it overly decorative
Do not use dark or black backgrounds
Do not use leaf, floral, or soft pastel elements
Focus on sharp structure, clean hierarchy, and vibrant professional colors
Make it look like a premium template someone would actually use in a serious presentation
